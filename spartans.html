Spartans

Civ Bonuses

Greek Olympians

Certain Champs and Special Infantry 3% faster walk speed, Melee units +5% damage, Ranged units +3% damage, 5% more accurate, and +1 range.

Hellenic Architecture

Civic Centers, Temples, Special Structures, and Wonders +10% health and +20% capture points.

Agoge

Champion Hoplites are available in phase 1 with -10% repeat time, as Young Hoplites (Paidiskoi) they cost -50 food and metal and take only 16s to train. However, they do 25% less damage, have -2 hack/pierce resistance and -25 health. They promote to normal Spartiates (Hebontes) which are available to train in Town Phase and can eventually promote to Olympic Spartiates (Hippeis).

Born To Fight

Melee infantry +5% more damage.

Perioeci Class

Melee and Ranged

Team Bonuses

Heroes train for free; Melee Infantry Heroes 15% more Pierce/Hack/Crush damage. Infantry +1 hack/pierce resistance and promote 10% faster.

Units

Heroes

Leonidas

Available town phase, but 1/2 health and 20% less dmg. Gains the health and dmg when you go city phase. Champion Infantry +15% melee attack, +1.5 hack/pierce, +5 Crush resistances and +25 health. Spearman 5% walk and 10% run speed. Available on p3 version only

Agis

Own and allied soldiers -40% metal cost and -25% training time. Agis 33% health and 5 of each resistance

Brasidas

Javelineers +2 crush, hack, pierce resistance, +3 capture attack. Range 60. Helots promote 50% faster to neodamodes
