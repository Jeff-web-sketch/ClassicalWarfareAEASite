Macedonians

Civilization Bonuses

Greek Olympians

Certain Champs and Special Infantry 3% faster walk speed, Melee units 5% more dmg while ranged units 3% dmg, 5% more accurate and +1 range.

Hellenistic Architecture

CCs, Temples, Special buildings and Wonders +10% health and +20% capture points.

Nobleman Cavalry

Cavalry 1 of Hack/Pierce resistance and 10% more dmg, but citizen cavalry cost +15 metal and take 15% longer to train.

Shepard army core

Citizen Pikeman and Archers -10% train time and cost 5 less food and wood but 5% less dmg. Pikeman -0.5 pierce/hack resistance while Archers is 10% less accurate. They lose conscript status when promoted to basic rank.

Phalangite core

Mace Foot Companions -0.5 Hack/Pierce, -1 Crush resistance but cost 5 less metal and -5% train time.

Resource-Rich Economy

Storehouse technologies -100% research time.

Nisean War Horses

Champion Cavalry +50 health, but 10% longer train time.

Team Bonuses
